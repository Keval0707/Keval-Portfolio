How I shape every design project

I move from a user question to a prototype the team can try, then document the details developers need.

01 / RESEARCH

Understand the problem before solutions

I start with the user’s task, look at the existing flow and ask what would make the next decision easier.

02 / DESIGN

Shape clear, considered product experiences

Concepts turn into flows, then into polished screens with a shared visual language.

03 / DEVELOP

Build with craft, clarity and precision

I stay close to engineering so what ships matches what was designed.

04 / HANDOFF

Ready for confident next steps

Specs, assets and documentation packaged so your team can keep moving fast.
