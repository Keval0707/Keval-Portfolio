About Keval

I start with a question.

What would someone need to know before tapping this button? Asking questions like that helps me turn a complicated task into a screen people can use with confidence.

I’m Keval Dhabalia, a UI/UX designer from Bardoli, Gujarat. I design web and mobile experiences at Intelliworkz in Ahmedabad. I like moving quickly from a rough idea to a prototype, because a few minutes using a screen can reveal more than another hour polishing it.
